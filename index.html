How the net spread is worked out
  1. Buy on the cheaper exchange at its best ask and sell on the other at its best bid, for the size shown.
  2. Gross spread = (sell price − buy price) ÷ buy price, in basis points (1 bp = 0.01 %).
  3. Subtract both taker fees, the extra slippage allowance per leg and the settlement (rebalancing) cost.
  4. A paper trade is placed only when the net spread clears the threshold and both books are fresh.
